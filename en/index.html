Health Check: a clear picture in three weeks

A short, independent review of how your company runs technology. It takes two to three weeks and has a fixed price.

You receive a plain-language report on:

  • Decisions: who decides what, and does the board get the facts it needs?
  • Safety: where are the biggest gaps?
  • IT service: how well does IT serve the business?
  • Daily work: where is work slow, manual or uncontrolled?
  • Automation and AI: where do they add value, and are the risks under control?

You also get a ranked list of actions for the next 90 days.

Our AI approach

We call our AI quality system an AI harness. It makes sure every AI result is good enough, and worth its cost.

It also means risks are spotted early and kept small, through clear policies, tools and controls.

ADLC means the Agentic Development Life Cycle: a clear method for planning, building, testing, releasing and running AI software.

How we work

Five simple steps, from first talk to real results.

  1. A free 30-minute call. You tell us what worries you most.

  2. A Health Check: a short review of how your company runs technology.

  3. A simple list of the most useful actions for the next 90 days, in order of importance.

  4. We make the changes with your team, one step at a time.

  5. We teach your team, so they can run the result on their own.

Who we are

Lee Solutions and Consulting Co., Ltd. ("we") is responsible for the personal data described here. You can reach us at info@leesc.in. Registered address: Bangkok, Thailand.

What this website collects

  • Contact form. The form on our home page does not send anything by itself. It opens your own email program with a message ready to send. We receive your name, email address, company, topic and message only if you press send in your email program.
  • Email you send us. If you email us directly, we receive your email address and whatever you write.
  • Cookies and tracking. We use no advertising tools. With your permission only, we use Google Analytics, which sets cookies to count visits to our pages, the pages viewed, and clicks on our email and LINE links. Google receives your browsing data and IP address (shortened) for this purpose, and may process it outside Thailand. Nothing is loaded until you press Accept, and you can change your choice at any time: .
  • Language choice. The English / Thai switch stores your choice only in your own browser, on your device. We do not receive it.
  • Hosting records. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records the IP address of visitors for security purposes (see GitHub's explanation).

Why we use your information

We use it to reply to your enquiry, to discuss and deliver our services if you become a client, and to keep normal business records.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for as long as needed to deal with your request and for our business records, and then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, as allowed by Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019). Email us at info@leesc.in.

Changes

Fractional CTO and Head of IT

Need a senior technology leader without hiring a full-time one? Call us when something is urgent, or when you want advice that follows recognised good practice. We step in as your Chief Technology Officer (CTO, the top technology leader) or Head of IT, for as long as you need us.

What we offer

Services in this area

Use us for one urgent job, for a few days each month, or as your standing technology leader until you hire your own.

Urgent problem solving

When a system fails, a project stalls or a supplier lets you down, we take the lead, find the real cause and agree the first steps with you.

Fractional CTO

Technology strategy, key technology choices and a roadmap tied to your business goals, for the days each month that suit you.

Head of IT

Run IT day to day: priorities, budget, team and suppliers, until you are ready to hire your own head of IT.

Digital transformation leadership

Lead the change from plan to results, and keep the business and IT working together. See our Digital Transformation service for the full approach.

Advice that follows good practice

Second opinions and reviews based on recognised good practice, written in plain words so your board can decide.

Supplier and contract oversight

Review quotes and contracts, question scope and price, and hold suppliers to what they promised.

How we deliver

Listen, stabilise, lead, hand over

  1. Step 1 Listen

    Tell us the problem or the goal. We ask the right questions and confirm what matters most.

  2. Step 2 Stabilise

    Deal with the urgent items first, so the business can keep running.

  3. Step 3 Lead

    Agree a regular rhythm: priorities, decisions, budget and reports.

  4. Step 4 Hand over

    Document the work and train or help hire the people who will carry on.

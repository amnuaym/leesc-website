Thailand Market Entry

Planning to sell in Thailand? We help international companies build a clear market expansion plan, then set up the channels and partners to carry it out. Our advice comes from hands-on experience with IT vendors in Thailand.

What we offer

Services in this area

Use us to write the plan, to find and manage partners, or both. We focus on technology companies, and also work with other international businesses.

Market entry plan

A clear plan for target customers, your offer, pricing and first-year priorities in Thailand.

Channel strategy

The right mix of direct sales, distributors, resellers, system integrators and online sales.

Partner search and selection

A shortlist of local partners, checked against clear criteria before you commit.

Partner programme and management

Clear rules, margins, targets and training, with regular reviews of results.

Multichannel coordination

Every channel pulls in the same direction, with fewer clashes over price and territory.

Launch and set-up support

First deals, a plan for your local team, and a handover to your own people. We work alongside your lawyers and accountants on company set-up.

How we deliver

Understand, plan, build, launch

  1. Step 1 Understand

    Learn your product, your goals and what you have already tried.

  2. Step 2 Plan

    Agree the target market, the channels and the first-year plan.

  3. Step 3 Build

    Select partners and set the rules they will work under.

  4. Step 4 Launch

    Win the first deals, review results and hand over to your team.
